Our story

From mint pioneers to specialty aroma chemicals

With Petorose Alcones, the Banwari Group commands a strong position in a niche segment of the industry, making single molecules accessible to manufacturers and innovators worldwide.

  1. Early 1970s

    Pioneering India’s mint industry

    The Banwari Group’s experienced management has its roots in the early 1970s, pioneering the mint industry in India.

  2. Banwari Chemicals

    Into specialty aroma chemicals

    The group forayed into specialty aroma chemicals with one mission: Innovating Aromas. Banwari Chemicals Pvt Ltd is today Petorose Alcones Pvt Ltd.

  3. A first for India

    Purest Anethole

    Petorose Alcones became India’s first manufacturer of purest Anethole, and with it took on the responsibility to build better.

  4. Today

    39 specialty aroma chemicals

    Single molecules made accessible to a wide network of perfumers, flavourists, manufacturers and innovators, with ‘Make in India’ at our core.

What drives us

Innovating Aromas

How we make it

Designed in the lab, made in Rajasthan

  1. 01

    Designed in the lab

    Our specialty aroma chemicals are designed in the lab to imitate the properties of their naturally occurring counterparts, adding longevity and strength in application.

  2. 02

    Developed by experts

    Every product is developed under the supervision of highly qualified scientists and lab experts.

  3. 03

    Made in Bhiwadi

    Manufactured in an all-compliant, international-grade facility in the RIICO Industrial Area, Bhiwadi, Rajasthan.

  4. 04

    Tested before it reaches you

    We rigorously test every product before it reaches you, and Kosher and Halal certification is available product by product.
